# 1. The niche: residential HVAC contractors

**Decision:** sell to owner-operated HVAC companies (3-30 trucks) in cold-winter US metros, starting now (late
October) because heating season is when their phones overflow.

**What we sell them:** recovered revenue, not more leads. We turn demand they already paid for (calls they miss,
quotes that never closed) into booked jobs. That's why results show up in days, not months, and days is how
fast you get paid.

## Why this offer and not ads/SEO

Every HVAC owner has been pitched SEO and Google Ads. Those take 60-120 days to prove anything and need ad
budget on top of your fee. Our two products monetize what they already have:

1. **Unsold estimates.** Every shop sends replacement quotes that never close. Those homeowners already said
   they need a system. Working that list is the fastest way to put appointments on a calendar, so it's our
   front-end offer with a guarantee.
2. **Missed and after-hours calls.** A full-time CSR covers 40 of the week's 168 hours (24%). When a furnace
   dies at 9pm, the homeowner calls whoever picks up. An AI dispatcher answers every call, triages emergencies
   and books into the schedule. That's the recurring revenue.

## Ideal client profile (what the engine scores for)

- Independent, owner-operated (not a franchise with a corporate call center)
- 50-1,000 Google reviews, rating 4.5+ (busy enough to miss calls, cares about reputation)
- Spending on Google Ads or Local Services Ads, or using call tracking (they already measure calls)
- No online booking or chat on the website
- Main line goes to voicemail after hours (the after-hours audit)
- Uses ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, FieldEdge or similar (easy unsold-estimate export)
- Sells replacements and offers financing (big tickets → fast ROI)
