# Social: Facebook groups, LinkedIn, Instagram

Social is a trust channel, not a spam channel. Post useful things where HVAC owners hang out, then DM the people
who engage. Never cold-DM a pitch with a link.

## Where HVAC owners are

- Facebook groups for HVAC business owners and contractors (search "HVAC business owners", "HVAC contractors",
  "HVAC owners"; join 5-10 with 5k+ members and real activity). Read each group's rules before posting.
- LinkedIn: search Owner / President / General Manager + "HVAC" or "Heating & Air" in your target metros.
- Instagram: local HVAC brand accounts (owner often runs it).

## Posts that start conversations (one per group per week, max)

**1. The after-hours experiment**
> I called 40 HVAC companies in {metro} last week between 6 and 8pm, from the number on their Google listing.
> {X} picked up. {Y} went to voicemail. {Z} had a phone menu that never reached a person.
> Not judging, I get that nobody wants to pay a CSR to sit there at night. Curious how you all handle after-hours
> calls in winter? On-call tech carries the phone? Answering service? Let it ring?

*(Only post numbers from audits you actually ran. The engine's audit log has them.)*

**2. The unsold-quote question**
> Honest question for owners: what happens to a replacement quote when the homeowner doesn't sign that day?
> Do you have a follow-up process that actually runs, or does it die in ServiceTitan?

**3. Show the work (once you have results)**
> Ran a 14-day campaign on an HVAC shop's old unsold quotes (last 18 months, {n} quotes).
> {booked} appointments booked, {sold} replacements sold so far, about ${revenue} in revenue from quotes they'd
> written off. What worked: {one specific lesson}. Happy to share the exact texts/emails if useful.

Reply to every comment. When someone engages, DM.

## DMs

**To someone who commented**
> Hey {name}, saw your comment on {post}. {Specific reaction to what they said.} Out of curiosity, what's your
> setup for calls after 5?

**When they share a pain point**
> That's super common. I've been building an AI dispatcher for HVAC shops that answers 24/7 and books into the
> schedule. Want to hear it? You can call the demo line and try to break it: {demo line}

**When they say it's interesting**
> Easiest thing is a 15-minute call. I'll show you where calls and quotes are leaking for {company} and you can
> decide if it's worth doing. Tomorrow 7:30 or Thursday 4:30?

## LinkedIn

**Connection note (no pitch)**
> Hi {name}, I work with HVAC companies on after-hours calls and unsold quotes. Always good to know more owners in
> {metro}.

**After they accept (wait a day)**
> Thanks for connecting, {name}. Quick question I've been asking owners: when a replacement quote doesn't close on
> the spot, does anyone at {company} follow up after the first week?

**If they reply** → move to the "when they share a pain point" DM above → book the call.

## Instagram (for shops whose owner runs the account)

Comment something real on 2-3 posts over a week (not "great work!"). Then:
> Hey, love the {specific post}. Random question: who handles your phones after hours in the winter rush?

## Rules

- Max 20 new DMs a day per platform, and only to people who've seen you engage first.
- No links in the first DM.
- Log anyone who becomes a real conversation: `bookedout mark <email-or-domain> --status interested`
  (import them first if they're not in the database).
