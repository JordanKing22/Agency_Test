## Offer 1: Bid List Sprint (front end)

**Promise:** "In 30 days we'll get {company} onto at least 10 new general contractors' bid lists in your market. If
we don't, you pay nothing."

**Price:** $2,500 flat. **Founding rate: $1,500 for the first 5 clients** in exchange for honest feedback, a
testimonial if it works, and permission to share anonymized results.

**What's included**
1. Kickoff call and document collection (COI, W-9, EMR letter, bonding letter, licenses, certifications, project list)
2. One-page capabilities statement (PDF) and an updated project list with GC references
3. Profiles set up or cleaned up on the bid platforms GCs use (BuildingConnected, PlanHub, ConstructConnect and
   similar), with correct trades, regions and project sizes so ITBs actually match
4. **Bid List Gap Audit:** the top 40 GCs by commercial permits in their market, marked against who already invites them
5. Target list of 60-100 GCs, with estimator / precon / procurement contacts
6. Outreach to those GCs as the client's business development rep (email from a mailbox on the client's domain,
   LinkedIn, phone)
7. Prequalification forms completed for GCs that require them (with the client's approval on every submission)
8. Weekly update and a day-30 report: GCs added, ITBs received, meetings set, next steps

**Qualification (protects your guarantee)**
- Commercial work is at least half their revenue
- Insurance, safety record and (where their work needs it) bonding capacity to take on new GCs
- They can send documents within 3 business days and approve outreach templates within 2
- No conflict: you don't already have a client in their trade in that metro

**Guarantee:** at least 10 new GCs confirm they've added the client to their bid list or sub database, accept their
prequal, or send an invitation to bid within 30 days of document receipt. Otherwise a full refund. Revisit the
number after your first 3 sprints.

## Offer 2: Bid Desk (core, recurring)

**Promise:** "We run business development and bid follow-up for {company}, so your estimators only bid work worth
winning and every bid gets chased."

**Price:** $2,500/month + $1,500 onboarding (waived for sprint graduates who start within 14 days). 3-month
minimum, then month-to-month. Quarterly prepay: 5% off.

**What's included**
- **New GC relationships every month:** 20+ new GCs contacted, prequals kept current (insurance, EMR, financials renewals)
- **ITB triage:** every invitation scored against the client's bid/no-bid rules (trade, size, GC history, location,
  schedule, bonding) → a weekly shortlist so estimators stop wasting takeoffs on jobs they won't get
- **Bid chasing:** confirm receipt and scope questions within a day of bid day; ask for leveling feedback at
  day 7; ask whether the GC won the project and who's running buyout at days 14-30; request the buyout meeting
- **Win/loss tracking and GC scorecard:** hit rate by GC, so the client knows who actually awards them work
- **Relationship touches:** quarterly check-ins with each GC's precon team, project-completion photos and
  references sent to GCs
- Monthly report and a 30-minute review

**Guarantee:** at least 25 new qualified invitations to bid (matching their bid/no-bid rules) in the first 90 days,
or the next month is free.

**The anchor to use on calls:** an in-house business development rep costs roughly $70k-$110k a year plus
benefits, takes months to ramp, and still won't chase bids. The Bid Desk is $30k a year and starts in week one.

## Offer 3: Bid Desk + Public Work (top)

$3,950/month. Everything in Bid Desk plus:
- Public bid sourcing: federal (SAM.gov), state, county, city, school district and transit opportunities that match
  their trade, plus plan holders lists so they can reach the GCs bidding those jobs
- Certification support: help preparing MBE/WBE/DBE/SBE applications and renewals (the client signs and submits)
- Quarterly GC lunch-and-learn or project tour coordination

Pitch this at the 90-day review to clients doing public or institutional work.

## Revenue model (planning assumptions, not a promise)

Assumptions to replace with your own numbers by week 3:
- Email live from day 15: ~6,000 sends/month → 3 booked sales calls per 1,000 sends
- Phone: 50 dials/day → ~10% reach an owner or chief estimator → ~10% of conversations book a call
- 70% of booked calls show; 20% of held calls buy (almost always the sprint first)
- 50% of sprints start the Bid Desk; ~1 Bid Desk cancellation by month 6

## Levers that make the money come faster

1. **Lead with the free Gap Audit.** It's specific, it's true, and it sells the sprint for you.
2. **Take payment on the call.** Stripe link in the chat before you hang up.
3. **Phone first.** Email needs ~2 weeks of warmup; owners and chief estimators answer at 6:30am.
4. **Exclusivity is real scarcity:** "We take one drywall sub per metro. Nobody in Austin has the spot yet."
5. **Quarterly prepay** at the 30-day review.
6. **Raise the sprint to $3,500** after 3 documented results.
7. **Referrals:** subs know subs in other trades on the same jobs. Pay $500 per referred client who signs.
