Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

[Legal business name] ("FullBacklog", "we", "us") provides business development and bid management services to commercial subcontractors. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

When you contact us or request an audit: your name, company, trade, metro, email, phone and anything you tell us. When we provide services to a client: business documents the client shares with us (insurance certificates, safety records, licenses, certifications, project lists and, if the client chooses, financial statements) and business contact information for general contractors. We also collect basic website usage data such as pages visited and browser type.

How we use it

To respond to your request, prepare audits, provide our services, communicate with general contractors on a client's behalf with the client's authorization, and meet legal obligations. Public records we use, such as building permits, are used as published.

Confidentiality of bid information

We never share one client's bid prices, documents or business information with another client or with any third party except as the client directs.

Sharing

We share information with service providers that help us operate (for example email, hosting and data providers) under obligations to protect it, with general contractors as a client directs, and when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Text messages

We do not send marketing text messages.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Client documents are stored in client-owned folders where possible, and we delete our copies within 30 days after an engagement ends.

Your choices
