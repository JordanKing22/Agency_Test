Why good subs run thin on backlog

It's rarely the work. It's who knows you exist.

△ 1 Invitations

You only see the jobs you're invited to

New GCs move into your market every year and pull permits you never hear about. If you're not in their bid list or BuildingConnected group, you never get the chance to price the work.

△ 2 Bid day

Nobody chases the bid after it goes out

Your estimator is on the next takeoff by the afternoon. Nobody asks where the number landed, whether the GC won the project, or who's running buyout, and that's where a lot of awards get decided.

△ 3 Concentration

Three GCs are most of your revenue

That works until one of them has a slow year. Every GC you add who actually awards you work makes next year's backlog less of a bet.

Do you email GCs as us?

Yes, from a mailbox on your domain, signed as your business development rep. You approve the templates and the target list first, and every fact we use comes from your own documents.

Do you estimate or bid for us?

No. We never touch your numbers. We get you invited, help your estimators decide what's worth pricing, and chase every bid after it's submitted.

Where does the GC list come from?

Public building permit records for your market, bid boards, association directories and your own wish list. You see who pulled the most commercial permits in the last 90 days and approve who we contact.

We tried BuildingConnected and got junk invitations.

That's usually an over-broad profile. We set your trades, counties and project sizes so invitations match, and the Bid Desk filters what's left.

Union or open shop?

Both. We only target GCs who use your labor type for your trade on the kind of work you do.

What if we're certified (MBE, WBE, DBE, SDVOSB)?

Even better. GCs with participation goals on public and institutional work go looking for certified subs, and we make sure they find you.
