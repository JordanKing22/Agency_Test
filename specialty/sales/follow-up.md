# Follow-up templates

Reply to every positive reply within 15 minutes during business hours. Send a promised Gap Audit the same day.

## Replies to cold email

**"Send the list" / "sure"**
> {First name}, here's the {market} list: top 25 GCs by commercial permits over the last 90 days, with their largest
> projects. [attach gap-audit markdown as PDF, or paste the table]
>
> If you mark which ones invite {company} to bid, I'll show you how we'd get you on the rest. 15 minutes {two times}?

**Positive ("how does it work?", "what does it cost?")**
> {First name}, short version: in 30 days we get {company} onto at least 10 new GC bid lists in {market}, or you don't
> pay. $2,500 ($1,500 for our first 5 clients), and we only take one {trade} sub per metro.
>
> Easiest way to see if it fits is 15 minutes on the GC list for {market}. {Two times}? Or grab a slot: {calendar link}

**"Not now"**
> No problem. When should I check back? I'll send the {market} GC list in the meantime so you have it.

**"Remove me"**
> Understood, I won't reach out again.
*(Then `fullbacklog mark <email> --status do_not_contact`.)*

## Booked a call

**Confirmation (immediately)**
> Subject: {Day} {time}: {company} + {agency}
>
> {First name}, confirmed for {day} at {time} ({time zone}). I'll call you at {phone} and share my screen with the GC
> list for {market}.
>
> If you can, have a rough list of the GCs who invite you today and how many bids you put out a month.
>
> {Your name}

**Reminder text (2 hours before):** "Hi {first name}, {your name} here. Still good for {time}? I'll call this number."

**No-show:** text at +5 minutes ("Tried you at {time}, want to grab {two times} instead?"), call once at +15, email the
same afternoon with the audit attached ("Here's the list either way").

## After the sales call

**Recap for "need to think about it" (within 1 hour)**
> Subject: {company}: the GC gap in {market}
>
> {First name}, thanks for the time. Recap:
> - You're getting ITBs from {4} of the top {15} GCs building in {market}. The {11} you're missing pulled about
>   {$value} in commercial permits this quarter. (Audit attached.)
> - About {20} bids a month at ~{12%}; average job ~{$120k}.
> - Nobody follows up after bid day today.
>
> What I'd recommend: the 30-day Bid List Sprint. {Price}, at least 10 new GC bid lists or you pay nothing, and the
> {trade} spot in {market} is yours while you're a client.
>
> Start here: {Stripe link}. Then a short onboarding form and we kick off {day}.
>
> We're scheduled to talk {day/time}.
>
> {Your name}

**Proposal cadence:** day 1 recap → day 2 text → day 4 call → day 7 email with one new data point (a big permit a
missing GC just pulled) → day 14 "should I offer the {trade} spot to someone else in {market}?"

## Won

**Welcome email**
> Subject: Welcome aboard: next steps for {company}
>
> {First name}, welcome! Next steps:
> 1. Onboarding form + document list (10 minutes): {form link}. Upload documents to the shared folder: {folder}
> 2. Kickoff call: {day/time}, 30 minutes. Please bring your list of current GCs and any you refuse to work for.
> 3. Weekly update every Friday; your day-30 report on {date}.

## Referrals and testimonials

**Testimonial (after a good sprint)**
> {First name}, glad we got you onto {n} new lists. Would you be up for 2 sentences I can share with other subs? What
> you expected and what happened. A 30-second phone video is even better.

**Referrals (other trades on the same jobs)**
> Which subs in other trades do you respect and see on the same jobs? If they sign up, we'll send you $500.
