# LinkedIn, associations and in-person

Commercial construction runs on relationships. LinkedIn is where owners and estimators are reachable; association
events are where subs and GCs are in the same room.

## LinkedIn

**Your profile:** headline "I get commercial subcontractors on more GC bid lists | {metro}". Banner: a cropped Gap Audit
table (blur the names if you prefer). Featured: one case study, once you have it.

**Who to connect with:** Owner, President, VP, Chief Estimator, Estimator, Director of Preconstruction at
drywall, electrical, glazing, concrete, fire protection, painting and flooring companies in your metros. 20-25 a day.

**Connection note (no pitch)**
> Hi {name}, I work with commercial {trade} subs in {metro} on bid lists and bid follow-up. Always good to know more
> people on the sub side here.

**After they accept (wait a day)**
> Thanks for connecting, {name}. I've been pulling the commercial permit data for {metro}; {top GC} has pulled more
> than anyone this quarter. Out of curiosity, are they one of the GCs {company} bids for?

**When they reply**
> Got it. I have the full top-25 list for {metro} by permits pulled. Happy to send it, and if you want I can walk you
> through it in 15 minutes so you can see which ones you're missing. {Two times}?

**Posts that start conversations (1-2 a week)**

1. *Market data (your best post, monthly):*
   > Commercial building permits issued in {metro}, last 90 days: {n} permits, about {$value}.
   > Top GCs by permit value: {1}, {2}, {3}, {4}, {5}.
   > Subs: how many of these invite you to bid?
   (Use real numbers from `fullbacklog markets`. Tag nobody. Cite "public permit records".)

2. *The follow-up question:*
   > Estimators: after bid day, how often do you find out where your number landed? Always / sometimes / never?

3. *Show the work (once you have results):*
   > 30 days with a {trade} sub in {metro}: {n} new GC bid lists, {n} ITBs, {n} bids submitted, first award in week {n}.
   > What moved the needle: {one specific lesson}.

Reply to every comment; DM people who engage.

## Associations and events

- **American Subcontractors Association (ASA)** chapters, **ABC** and **AGC** chapters, and trade associations in your
  target trades (wall and ceiling, painting, glazing, electrical, fire sprinkler, concrete) all have local events.
- Go to one a month. Bring printed Gap Audits for your metro (top 25 GCs, no "invites you" column).
- Opener at events: "What trade are you in? ... Who are the GCs you bid for most?" Then: "I just pulled the permit
  data for {metro}. Want to see who's building the most right now?" Hand them the page, get their card, follow up
  the next morning.
- GCs at those events are your future delivery contacts. Meet their estimators and precon people; it makes every
  sprint easier.

## Rules

- No links in a first DM.
- Max 25 connection requests and 20 new DMs a day.
- Log real conversations: `fullbacklog mark <email-or-domain> --status interested` (import them first if needed).
