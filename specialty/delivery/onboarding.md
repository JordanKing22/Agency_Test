# Client onboarding

## The moment they pay

- [ ] `fullbacklog mark <lead> --status won`
- [ ] Welcome email (`sales/follow-up.md`) with the onboarding form and shared-folder link
- [ ] Agreement sent for e-signature: scope, fees, guarantee terms, authorization to represent them to GCs, use of
      their mailbox and platform logins, confidentiality of bid information, one-sub-per-trade-per-metro exclusivity,
      data deletion on termination
- [ ] Kickoff booked within 48 hours
- [ ] Market marked as taken for their trade (keep a simple exclusivity list: trade × metro → client)

## Onboarding form

**Company**
1. Legal name, DBA, address, website, years in business, office and field headcount
2. Owner, chief estimator and estimating email (where ITBs should go)
3. Trades and CSI divisions; scope they self-perform vs. sub out
4. Service area (counties/cities); willing to travel for the right job?
5. Union or open shop

**Work they want**
6. Project types (TI, ground-up, healthcare, education, industrial, multifamily, hospitality, government...)
7. Sweet-spot package size; smallest and largest they'll price
8. Bonding capacity (single/aggregate) and surety; insurance limits
9. EMR (last 3 years); safety program
10. Certifications and expiry dates

**GCs**
11. GCs that invite them today (name, how often, roughly how often they win)
12. GCs they refuse to work for, and why
13. GCs they'd most like to work for

**Operations**
14. Bids per month and estimators' capacity
15. Bid platforms used and who holds the logins
16. Who approves outreach and prequal submissions, and how fast

## Kickoff agenda (30 minutes)

1. Goals at day 30 and day 90, written down with numbers
2. Walk the Gap Audit together and mark known / refused / dream GCs
3. Approve the outreach templates and signature
4. Confirm documents and logins; set the approval turnaround (48 hours max)
5. Agree the weekly update (Friday) and who's on it

## Bid Desk start (after the sprint)

| Week | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Build the bid log and GC scorecard from the last 6 months of their bids (export from their email/platforms) |
| 1 | Agree bid/no-bid weights with the chief estimator; triage the backlog of open ITBs |
| 2 | Start bid-day follow-ups on everything submitted in the last 30 days |
| 3 | First monthly target list from fresh permit data; new GC outreach continues |
| 4 | First monthly report and review |

## Offboarding

Hand over the bid log, GC scorecard and target lists; return or delete document copies within 30 days; remove your
access to mailboxes and platforms; free the trade × metro slot. Ask for honest feedback and leave the door open.
