# GC outreach templates (sent on the client's behalf)

Sent from a mailbox on the client's domain, signed "{Your name}, Business Development, {Client}", with the client's
address and an opt-out line. The client approves these templates at kickoff. Every fact comes from the client's
documents.

Columns from the enriched `gc-targets-*.csv`: `{gc_first_name}`, `{gc_company}`, `{gc_title}`, plus the client
facts below.

---

## Email 1: add us to your bid list

> **Subject:** {trade} sub for {gc_company}'s {market} bid list
>
> Hi {gc_first_name},
>
> I handle business development for {client}, a {trade} contractor in {market} ({years} years, {crew_size} field
> staff, {certifications if any}). We'd like to be on {gc_company}'s bid list for {trade} on {project types} work in the
> {package range} range.
>
> Recent work: {project 1} with {GC A}, {project 2} with {GC B}, {project 3} with {GC C}. EMR {emr}, bonding up to
> {bonding}. Capabilities sheet attached.
>
> Who's the right person to get us into your sub database or prequal process?
>
> {Your name}
> Business Development, {client} · {phone}
> {client address} · Reply "remove" and I won't follow up.

## Email 2 (day 3, in thread)

> Hi {gc_first_name}, following up. If it's easier, I can fill out your prequal form or set up our
> BuildingConnected/PlanHub profile however you need it. Is there a {trade} package coming up we should be looking at?

## Email 3 (day 7, in thread): give them a reason

> {gc_first_name}, saw {gc_company} pulled permits on {their recent project from the permit data}. If {trade} isn't
> bought out yet, we'd like to price it. Happy to turn a number quickly.

Use only real permit records (address/description from the `gc-targets` file); don't guess at project status.

## Email 4 (day 14): close the loop

> {gc_first_name}, I'll stop following up. If you ever need another {trade} number in {market}, our estimating email is
> {estimating_email}. Capabilities attached again for your files.

---

## Phone: GC estimating line

> "Hi, this is {your name} with {client}, a {trade} sub here in {market}. Who handles adding new subs to your bid list
> for {trade}?"

To the estimator / precon person:
> "We do {project types} {trade} in the {package range} range: recent work with {GC A} and {GC B}. What does it take to
> get on your list? Is there a prequal form or a BuildingConnected group I should be in?"

If they're receptive:
> "Do you have anything coming up where you're short on {trade} coverage? We can turn a number fast."

## LinkedIn (to GC estimators / precon)

> Hi {gc_first_name}, I do business development for {client}, a {trade} sub in {market}. We'd like to price your
> {trade} packages. Mind if I send our capabilities?

## Intro meeting request (warm GCs)

> {gc_first_name}, thanks for adding us. Would 15 minutes with {client owner} be useful, either a quick call or coffee at
> your office? They can walk you through recent {project type} work and how they staff jobs. {Two times}?

## Log everything

Every "added you" / "prequal accepted" / ITB goes in the sprint tracking sheet with the email or screenshot as proof.
That's what the day-30 report and the guarantee rest on.
