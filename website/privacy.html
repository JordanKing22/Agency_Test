Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

[Legal business name] ("BookedOut", "we", "us") provides call answering, scheduling and customer follow-up services to home service companies. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

When you contact us or request an audit: your name, company, phone number, email, city and anything you tell us. When we provide services to a home service company: information their customers share on calls, texts, emails and forms (name, phone, service address, details about their equipment and appointment). We also collect basic website usage data such as pages visited and browser type.

How we use it

To respond to your request, provide and improve our services, schedule appointments, send service-related messages, and meet legal obligations. Information we process for a home service company is used only to provide services to that company and its customers.

Text messaging

If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number to send messages related to your request or appointment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

We share information with service providers that help us operate (for example telephone, messaging, scheduling and hosting providers) under obligations to protect it, with the home service company you contacted, and when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Call recording

Calls answered by our virtual assistant may be recorded and transcribed for quality and to complete your request. Callers are told this at the start of the call.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we protect it with reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards.

Your choices
